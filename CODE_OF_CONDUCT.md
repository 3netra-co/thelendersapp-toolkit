# Code of conduct

We welcome respectful contributions from people of every background and level
of experience.

Be constructive, discuss the work rather than the person, respect privacy, and
do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private
information. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@thelenders.app](mailto:support@thelenders.app).
